What This Service Covers

Same-Day Means Same-Day Inspection — Honest About What It Does and Doesn't Mean

Same-day rat removal is priority-dispatch rat service where a technician arrives for inspection and initial treatment on the same calendar day you call. In Waco and McLennan County, most calls placed before noon reach a same-day appointment — we confirm the window before you hang up. Same-day service starts the treatment clock immediately, which matters because rat damage is ongoing: gnawed wiring, contaminated insulation, and expanding entry points don't pause while a standard scheduling queue works through backlog.

We're honest about one thing upfront: same-day service means same-day inspection and trap deployment. It does not mean rats are gone same-day. Active rat removal — getting population below detectable activity — takes 2–4 weeks of trap monitoring regardless of how fast we arrive. What same-day gives you is an immediate start rather than losing a week or more before anyone opens the first trap.

What "Same-Day" Looks Like in Practice

  • Call — Before Noon
    Triage and Dispatch

    You call (254) 343-1352 and describe what you're seeing — droppings, sounds, gnaw marks, location on the property. We confirm same-day availability, give you an arrival window (typically 2–4 hour range), and collect the address and access notes. No hold music, no callback queue.

  • Same Day — Arrival
    Inspection and Species Confirmation

    Technician arrives with full inspection and treatment kit. Whole-property walkthrough — attic, crawl, exterior perimeter, interior evidence. Norway rat or roof rat confirmed from droppings, location, and runway evidence before any treatment recommendation is made.

  • Same Day — Before We Leave
    Written Quote and Trap Deployment

    Scope and price presented before any traps are set. Your agreement, then trap deployment on confirmed runways. You're briefed on what was placed and where. Follow-up visit scheduled before we leave — typically 5–7 days out.

  • Days 5–21
    Follow-Up Visits and Knockdown

    Follow-up trap checks at 5–7 day intervals. Catch tracking, fresh-dropping audit, trap repositioning as needed. Activity typically drops noticeably after the first follow-up; resolution confirmed when evidence clears across two consecutive visits.

  • Post-Resolution
    Exclusion and 30-Day Warranty

    Entry-point sealing after active knockdown is confirmed. 30-day return warranty from resolution date. If you need cleanup or insulation assessment, that's scoped as a separate add-on visit.

No — and any company that claims otherwise is not being honest. Same-day service means same-day inspection and initial trap deployment.
