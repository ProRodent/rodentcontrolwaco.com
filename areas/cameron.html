Rodent Pressure Profile

Cameron — Milam County Seat in the Central Texas Agricultural Belt

Cameron is the Milam County seat approximately 50 miles southeast of Waco on US-79. Milam County is characterized by rich agricultural land — black-land prairie cotton and grain operations, cattle ranches, and the Little River corridor that creates riparian Norway rat habitat throughout the county. Cameron's residential neighborhoods span pre-war historic construction through contemporary suburban development, with the rodent pressure profile shifting accordingly: older pier-and-beam homes in the historic sections with complex crawl-space entry-point inventories, post-war slab-on-grade with mid-century gap accumulation, and newer construction with builder-grade vulnerabilities. The Little River and its tributaries adjacent to Cameron create Norway rat water-edge pressure for riverside and creek-adjacent residential properties comparable to what Brazos-adjacent Waco neighborhoods face. Same-day service is available for most Cameron calls placed before noon; the US-79 drive from Waco is approximately 55 minutes.

Little River Corridor and Agricultural Coverage in Milam County

Cameron's position in the Little River watershed means flood-season Norway rat displacement is a real seasonal pressure event for river-adjacent properties — similar in character to the Brazos-corridor dynamic in East Waco, though the Little River's flood frequency and scale differs from the Brazos. Pre-spring inspection for Cameron properties with prior Norway rat activity is the same February timing we recommend for all river-adjacent Central Texas properties. Agricultural property service for Milam County farm operations is available at the same scope as our McLennan County agricultural program.
