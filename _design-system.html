FAQ

Do you offer same-day service in Waco?

Yes — most Waco-area calls placed before mid-afternoon can be inspected the same day, depending on technician routing. Call (254) 343-1352 to confirm next available slot.

Are your treatments safe around pets and children?

Our standard approach leads with mechanical trapping and exclusion. Any chemical product used follows Texas structural pest control regulations and is placed in tamper-resistant stations away from living-area access.

How long does a typical Waco rodent job take?

Initial inspection runs 45–90 minutes. Active treatment typically resolves over 1–3 follow-up visits across 2–4 weeks, depending on infestation severity and property size.
