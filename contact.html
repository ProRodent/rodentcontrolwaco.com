Best Times to Call

We answer 24/7, but routing is fastest on weekday mornings (6 AM–noon) and weekday afternoons (1–6 PM) when full crew availability is online. After-hours and weekend calls still get same-day response, but expect a brief routing pause if multiple emergencies are active.

What to Mention When You Call

A 30-second description lets us route faster and bring the right gear:

  • Your neighborhood (Baylor, Woodway, Downtown, etc.)
  • What you're seeing/hearing (droppings, scratching, dead-rodent smell)
  • Where on the property (attic, kitchen, crawl space, exterior)
  • Whether it's residential, rental, commercial, or agricultural
  • Any prior treatment attempted
