Riesel's Cotton, Sorghum, and Hay Operation Mix

Riesel's surrounding agricultural land includes cotton, sorghum, and hay operations with different rat-pressure timing profiles. Cotton harvest in October-November and sorghum harvest in summer both produce displacement spikes that affect adjacent properties. Hay operations sustain year-round Norway rat populations in stored bales regardless of season. Properties with multiple-operation adjacency face overlapping pressure cycles that benefit from coordinated bait-station programs rather than reactive single-event treatment. Annual scoping reviews the current adjacency pattern as operations change.

Hay, Sorghum, and Stored Feed Near Riesel Homes

With crops and hay close to the house, rodents are never far. Check how feed, seed, and stored grain are kept, and keep them in containers with tight lids. Prevention, as EPA frames it, starts with cutting off food, water and shelter and sealing garbage in tight containers. The 30 foot trash can distance comes from AgriLife Extension and applies here too. Look at the gap under doors and at the base of walls for burrows. Tell us about every building where you have seen signs, since a farm property often has more than one place to check.
